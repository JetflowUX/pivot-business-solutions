Pivot · Who I help

Different people, the same turning point.

Three groups come to me. What they have in common is the moment: something has to change, and the way forward is not obvious yet.

01

Athletes

In-season, injured, or moving on from the game. The sport gave you a structure, an identity, and a schedule that decided most of your week. When any of those changes, everything else moves with it.

We work on the inner game and the outer one in the same room: how you handle pressure and how you handle the decision in front of you.

  • Performance pressure, confidence, and the weeks after a bad result
  • Injury, rehabilitation, and the fear of not coming back the same
  • Identity when the sport stops being the answer to “what do you do?”
  • Contracts, brand, and the next career, planned properly rather than panicked into
Start with counseling →
02

Young people

Pressure, purpose, and the weight of other people's expectations. Sometimes the person who books the first conversation is a parent or a guardian. That is completely normal, and the young person is still the client.

A steady, confidential space to work out who you are separately from how you are performing. Nothing here is reported back, graded, or added to a file.

  • Expectation from family, school, coaches, or a team
  • Motivation, focus, and the difference between the two
  • Choosing a direction without closing every other door
  • Confidence, comparison, and the version of you that lives online
Start with counseling →
03

Businesses

Founders and small teams at a crossroads. The decision is usually already visible; what is missing is a clear reading of the options and someone outside the room who will say the honest thing.

We map the choices, weigh what each one actually costs, and build a plan you can move on. Then I stay in your corner while you execute it.

  • A direction that worked once and has stopped working
  • Growth that is outrunning how the business is organised
  • Operations, roles, and decisions that keep landing on one person
  • A choice between two roads, where standing still is the real risk
