Pivot · Approach

How a pivot actually happens.

A pivot keeps one foot planted while the other changes direction. That is the whole idea: move forward without losing your footing.

The method

Four steps, in order.

The same four steps carry the counseling work and the consulting work. Only the subject changes.

01

Ground

We start with where you actually are, honestly, including the parts that are uncomfortable to say out loud. No plan survives if it is built on a flattering version of the situation.

For a person that means the pressure, the fear, and what you actually want. For a business it means the numbers, the constraints, and which problems are real rather than inherited.

02

Assess

We map the real options and what each one costs. Not a list of everything possible, but the three or four roads genuinely open to you, with the trade-offs written down plainly.

Most people arrive believing they have one option, or none. Almost nobody has one.

03

Redirect

You choose. I will tell you what I think, and I will tell you when I think you are avoiding something, but the decision is yours and it needs to be one you can defend to yourself at 3am.

Then we turn it into a plan: concrete next steps, in an order, with the first one small enough to start this week.

04

Move

You execute, and I stay in your corner while real life pushes back. Plans meet conditions nobody predicted; the point of staying involved is to adjust without abandoning the direction.

This is the step most advice skips, and it is the one that decides whether anything actually changes.

The pivot isn't the end of the play. It's the start of the next one.

What to expect

Plain language, and a next move.

  • The first conversation is free and carries no obligation
  • A reply within one business day
  • In person or online, whichever you will actually keep
  • You leave every session with something specific to do

What this is not

No scripts, no packages, no pressure.

  • No programme you have to buy into before we have spoken
  • No motivational performance; the work is quieter than that
  • Nothing you say is reported to a coach, school, club, or board
  • If you need something I cannot offer, I will say so early

Step one is a conversation.

It costs nothing and commits you to nothing. Tell me roughly where you are and we will work out whether this is the right help.
