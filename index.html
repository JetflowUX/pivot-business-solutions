The method

How a pivot actually happens.

A pivot keeps one foot planted while the other changes direction. That is the whole idea: move forward without losing your footing.

  1. 01

    Ground

    We start with where you actually are, honestly. No plan works until we are standing on solid, shared ground.

  2. 02

    Assess

    We map the real options and the trade-offs behind each, so the choice ahead is clear instead of overwhelming.

  3. 03

    Redirect

    You choose the new direction with confidence, and we shape it into a plan with concrete next steps.

  4. 04

    Move

    You execute, and I stay in your corner, adjusting as real life pushes back.
