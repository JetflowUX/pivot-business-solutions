Pivot · Counseling

Life & professional counseling.

One-to-one work with athletes and young people on the part nobody sees: pressure, identity, and the decisions underneath the performance.

What this is

A room where you can say the real thing.

Most people arrive able to describe the problem to everyone except themselves. Counseling here is a steady, private conversation where you get to be honest first, and strategic second.

It is practical rather than theoretical. You leave with something you can do, not just something you understand.

What it is not

Not a lecture, and not a performance review.

Nothing you say is reported to a coach, a parent, a school, or a club. There is no scoring, no homework you have to hand in, and no requirement to arrive with your thoughts already organised.

If what you need is outside what I can offer, I will say so early and help you find the right person.

The work

What we actually work on.

Performance & pressure

Managing nerves, expectation, and the weeks after a bad result. How to keep a difficult stretch from becoming the story you tell about yourself.

Identity & transition

Who you are when the sport pauses, ends, or changes shape. Retirement, injury, being dropped, or simply outgrowing the thing that defined you.

Purpose & direction

Relationships, motivation, and choosing what comes next on your own terms rather than to satisfy the people watching.

How sessions run

  • In person or online, whichever you will actually turn up to
  • The first conversation is free and carries no obligation
  • We agree what you want out of it before we agree a schedule
  • For clients under 18, a parent or guardian sets things up; the sessions themselves stay confidential

There is no fixed package you have to buy into. Some people come for a handful of conversations around one decision. Others stay through a whole season.
