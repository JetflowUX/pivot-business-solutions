Part counselor, part strategist. Fully in your corner.

Life and business rarely sit in separate boxes, so this practice does not treat them that way.

The practice

I work with young people and athletes on the inner game: pressure, identity, and direction. I work with athletes and businesses on the outer one: the career moves, the brand, and the strategy.

Most providers do one or the other. You get a counselor who cannot advise on a career move, or a consultant with no standing to talk about who you are underneath the decision. For the person actually living it, those are the same problem seen from two sides, and splitting them across two people loses the thread.

Everything is confidential, judgment-free, and grounded in where you actually want to go. You bring the drive. Together we find the direction.

What holds across every engagement

Three commitments.

Confidential by default

What is said here stays here. Nothing is reported back to a coach, a club, a school, or a board, and this site stores nothing you type into it.

Athlete-informed

The pressures of performance are understood first-hand rather than from a textbook: the schedule, the scrutiny, and what it costs to keep showing up.

Practical, not theoretical

You leave with a next move, not just an insight. If a conversation does not change what you do on Monday, it has not finished.
