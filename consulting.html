Strategic guidance for the career, the brand, and the company. We map the options, weigh the risk, and build a plan you can actually move on.

For athletes

A playing career is a business with a short window and a lot of people advising you at once. The job is to separate the offers that build something from the ones that just pay this month.

  • Career planning across the whole arc, not just the current contract
  • Brand, sponsorship, and which opportunities are worth your name
  • Transition planning, started early enough to have real options
  • Decision support when several advisors are telling you different things

Because I also do the counseling work, we can talk about what a decision costs you personally, not only what it pays.

For businesses

Founders and small teams who can see the crossroads clearly but cannot get a clean read on the options. Usually the blocker is not information. It is that everyone in the room has a stake in one answer.

  • Strategy when the current direction has stopped paying off
  • Operations and structure that have not kept pace with growth
  • Decision support at the fork, with the trade-offs written down honestly
  • A plan with owners and dates, not a document that gets filed

I stay involved while you execute. A plan nobody adjusts is a plan that fails on contact with a real week.

Short, honest, and built to be acted on.

How an engagement runs

Three stages, in order
  • 01 — A free first conversation

    We work out whether this is the right help at all. If it is not, I will tell you, and point you somewhere better.

  • 02 — Scope before cost

    We agree what the engagement covers and what a good outcome looks like before anyone talks about a number.

  • 03 — In your corner

    Delivery is the plan plus the follow-through: staying reachable while the decision meets real conditions.

Fees are not published on this site. Nothing about pricing, retainers, or engagement length has been supplied by the practice, so nothing is stated here. Scope and cost are agreed in the first conversation.
